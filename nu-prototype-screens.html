Design principles

01Nu carries the admin

The work of collecting details, reminding and checking sits with Nu, not with Ana or her recipient. Fewer screens, plainer words.

02No dead ends

If an email or number doesn’t find an account, Ana goes straight to the amount and Nu gets the details. A miss is never a stop.

03The message comes from Ana

People trust a link from someone they know. Ana shares a ready-made message herself; Nu never texts recipients, and updates by email are opt-in.

04Only show what we know

No guessed names or numbers on screen. Until Ana names someone, it’s “To Brazil · BRL”; an account card appears only when a lookup can vouch for it.

05What they receive stays fixed

The amount the recipient gets is the constant. If the rate moves, Ana sees what it now costs — she never has to remember what she meant to send.

06Clear costs, no surprises

The mid-market rate with no markup, one fee shown on its own, limits before typing, and gentle guidance when something needs to change.

07Checked before you commit

Balance, quote and limits are checked live on Review, so Face ID is only offered when the transfer can go through. Trusted recipients skip Face ID.

08Kind, blame-free language

Words that reassure rather than burden: “You’re all set,” not “Waiting for Ana.” Celebrate the moment money lands.
